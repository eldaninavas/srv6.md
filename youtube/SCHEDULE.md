## The stories (the chain of "but…")

**l01 – You built the internet** (#internet)
Two cans and a string to your best friend → more kids, strings everywhere →
one box in the middle → your cousin's block, one long cable → a truck snaps
it → a second cable the long way → millions of boxes, messages with an
address. Reveal: routers, fiber, the internet.

**l02 – Why your call doesn't drop in the car** (#5g #cellphone)
You call grandma from the car → your phone talks to one tower → you drive
away, the signal fades → towers placed like a honeycomb, each covers a patch
→ your phone listens to the next tower before the first one fades → the
network moves your call over → a tunnel: no sky, so small antennas inside
it. Reveal: cells, handover, the "cell" in cellphone.

**l03 – How a message crosses the ocean** (#internet #ocean)
Your cousin moved across the ocean → a letter takes weeks by ship → 1858:
a cable on the sea floor, it dies after three weeks → better cables, the
signal needs a boost every few dozen km → glass fiber, light → a ship's
anchor cuts it (not sharks: anchors and fishing do most of the damage) → a
repair ship hooks it up → many cables, so traffic takes another one. Reveal:
over 95% of intercontinental traffic runs on undersea cables.

**l04 – Why you have no signal at a concert** (#5g #concert)
Full bars, but your video won't send → a tower is like one cashier → 50,000
fans post at once → the line explodes → operators drive in towers on trucks
→ hide small antennas around the stadium → more cells, fewer people per
cell. Reveal: capacity, "cells on wheels".

**l05 – How Wi-Fi reaches your room** (#wifi)
The router is in the living room, you are in your room → the signal is
radio, walls eat it → the microwave runs and your video stutters (same
2.4 GHz) → you move the router to the middle of the house → still weak
upstairs: a second little router that relays it → the neighbors' Wi-Fi
crowds the same channel → switch to 5 GHz: faster, shorter. Reveal: Wi-Fi
is a tiny radio station in your house.

**l06 – Why your show loads instantly** (#netflix #internet)
You press play → the show sits in a giant data center far away → every
viewer pulls it across the country, roads jam → so the company puts a copy
in a box right at your internet provider → you watch from a few km away →
the new episode drops at midnight, everyone at once, still smooth.
Reveal: content delivery networks (Netflix's boxes are called Open Connect).

**l07 – Where your photo goes when you hit send** (#internet #whatsapp)
You send a photo to a friend → too big to go in one piece → cut into
hundreds of small numbered envelopes → each finds its own way, some take
other roads → one gets lost → your friend's phone notices the missing
number and asks again → puts them back in order. Reveal: packets.
