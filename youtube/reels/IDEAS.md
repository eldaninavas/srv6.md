# Everyday networking stories (g-series)

Each one starts with something anyone does every day, then builds the
network that makes it possible, step by step, and ends tying it to how
networks work (srv6.md). Done: g01.

| id | hook | what it teaches |
|----|------|-----------------|
| g01 | Your cousin lives across the ocean. | Submarine fiber, redundancy, rerouting |
| g02 | You press play on a series. Why doesn't it buffer? | CDNs and caches close to you |
| g03 | You type a website name. How does your phone find it? | DNS, the internet's phone book |
| g04 | You walk out of the house mid-call. Why doesn't it drop? | Wi-Fi to cell handover, cell towers |
| g05 | Why does a video call freeze but a download doesn't? | Latency vs bandwidth, jitter |
| g06 | A whole stadium posts at once. Why does the signal die? | Cell capacity, why 5G adds cells |
| g07 | Your package goes through the same busy road every day. | Hop-by-hop routing vs choosing the path |
| g08 | One road closes and the whole city jams. | Redundancy and fast reroute |
| g09 | A million people want the same video at 9 pm. | Multicast vs unicast, caching |
| g10 | Your message to a friend next door goes through another city. | Peering and internet exchanges |
| g11 | How does your bank send money in a blink, safely? | Encryption, private networks (VPN) |
| g12 | Why does gaming lag feel worse than slow downloads? | Latency, distance, the speed of light |
| g13 | How does the internet keep working when a whole region fails? | Routing protocols, many paths |
| g14 | How does a chatbot answer you in a second? | Data centers, GPUs talking over networks (AI fabrics) |
| g15 | Why do addresses run out? | IPv4 exhaustion, why IPv6 exists |
| g16 | Your phone's data travels further than you'd think. | Access, aggregation, core, backbone |
